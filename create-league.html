GROUND RULES.

Keep rules, venues and schedules clear before inviting players to take part.

  1. 01

    Choose the format and area

    Decide whether you are organising singles, doubles or team play, and identify the players and venues that could take part.

  2. 02

    Agree the details in advance

    Confirm rules, venue access, match schedule, organiser responsibilities, entry costs and any prizes with participants before publishing them.

  3. 03

    Keep information accurate

    Share fixtures, results and table positions only after the league organiser has verified them. Make sure players know who to contact with questions.
