A GOOD PLACE TO START

GET TO THE
TABLE.

League availability depends on local organisers. Confirm dates, rules and entry details directly before committing.

  1. 01

    Look for verified local information

    Check the fixtures and league tables on the homepage when they are published. You can also ask pool venues and league organisers in your area which competitions currently accept players.

  2. 02

    Choose a format that suits you

    Ask whether the competition is singles, doubles or team play, and confirm the playing rules, schedule, venue and any entry costs with its organiser.

  3. 03

    Join or request a place

    Use the league listing to join immediately when open joining is enabled, or send a request for an organiser to review. Check the league details for its policy.

Check with the organiser

Joining a league does not create a season, team, fixture or score record. Confirm competition rules and schedules with its organiser.
